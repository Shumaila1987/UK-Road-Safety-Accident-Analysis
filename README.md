# UK-Road-Safety-Accident-Analysis
🚗 UK Road Safety Accident Analysis
 
Hi there! 👋 I'm Shumaila, a data analyst based in the UK. This is one of my recent projects where I explored UK road accident data to find interesting patterns and trends.
 
 
 
📌 What This Project Is About
 
I took a dataset with over 1 million road accident records and used Excel to clean it, analyze it, and create some simple charts to understand what's going on. It's amazing what you can learn just by looking at the numbers!
 
🛠️ What I Used
 
- Microsoft Excel — Pivot tables, charts, and good old data cleaning
 
🔍 What I Found Out
 
📈 Accident Trends Over Time
 
Accident numbers have actually been going down a bit over the years — which is great news for road safety!
 
📅 Which Day Has the Most Accidents?
 
Friday takes the top spot ⚠️ — probably everyone rushing home for the weekend. Sunday is the safest and quietest day ✅ — makes sense, fewer people on the roads early in the week.
 
🛣️ Which Roads Are Most Dangerous?
 
Single carriageways had by far the highest number of accidents — almost half of all incidents. Interestingly, motorways were actually the safest type of road despite how busy they get.
 
🕐 What Time of Day Is Riskiest?
 
5 PM was the clear peak ⚠️ — evening rush hour. Things quieten down after that, and between 2 AM and 4 AM you're safest on the roads — hardly any accidents at all then 🌙
